About This Trial

The National Centre of Excellence (NCOE) Athletics Selection Trials are conducted to identify talented athletes for admission into Sports Authority of India residential training centres.

Selected athletes receive advanced coaching, scientific support, sports infrastructure, nutrition guidance, accommodation and educational assistance under the NCOE programme.
